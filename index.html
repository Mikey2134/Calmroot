Come back to the present

The 5–4–3–2–1 grounding exercise

Move through these gently. You can keep your eyes open and skip any step that doesn't suit you.

  1. 5 things you can see around you.
  2. 4 things you can feel (your feet on the floor, fabric, or the chair).
  3. 3 things you can hear.
  4. 2 things you can smell, or two scents you like to imagine.
  5. 1 thing you can taste, or one kind thing you can tell yourself.

You don't have to get it perfect. If focusing on senses feels uncomfortable, simply name where you are and today's date.

Your privacy matters

Your private space

When you use Google sign-in, CALMROOT uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to sync your profile, mood check-ins and journal notes to your user account. Preview mode stays in this browser and does not sync.

  • Firestore rules are intended to let each signed-in user access only their own records.
  • The project owner administers the Firebase project and may technically access database records through project-level tools, so do not enter information you would not want the service operator to handle.
  • Export creates a JSON file on your device. Keep it somewhere private.
  • Deleting cloud data requires an internet connection. Clearing browser data does not delete cloud records.
  • This is a wellbeing tool, not a medical service. Avoid diagnoses or highly sensitive medical details.

Cloud sync requires Firebase Authentication and Firestore to be configured and available. Firebase free quotas apply; no paid storage service is used.
